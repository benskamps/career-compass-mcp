# What moves the odds: sourced numbers

These are the only hiring statistics Career Compass quotes. Quote one only when it changes
what the user does next, name its source in a few words ("Ashby's hiring data", "a
randomized study"), and never present it as a promise about their search. Don't quote
any other hiring statistic from memory; the famous ones are folklore (below).

Researched 2026-10-12. Full sources and method notes: the "After You Click Submit" research
page in the Career Compass project.

## Applying

| Lever | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cold application baseline | About 3% of cold applications reach an interview; about 1 in 200 to 300 gets an offer | Ashby, 38M applications, 2021–24; Gem, 165M applications, 2021–25 |
| Referral | 40% of referred candidates reach an interview, about 13x a cold application | Ashby, 2025 |
| Apply early | Applied on day 1: 8.56% matched; month-old postings: 4.64% | Wellfound platform data, 2025 |
| Tailor | Tailored résumés 5.8% reach interview vs 3.73% untailored | Huntr, 2025 (tracker users) |
| Company site | Career-page applicants are 13% of applicants but 26% of hires at small businesses | CareerPlug, 2024 data |
| Broaden the search | Showing job seekers related occupations raised interviews about 44%, and about doubled them for people searching narrowly | Belot, Kircher & Muller, randomized study, 2019 |
| Make a plan | A written weekly plan raised applications 18%, offers 30% | Abel et al., randomized study, 2019 |
| Employment gaps | Listing years of experience instead of exact dates raised callbacks about 15% over a résumé with a visible gap | Kristal et al., Nature Human Behaviour, 2023 (UK audit study) |
| Knockout questions | Screening questions on hard requirements are the one documented way applicant systems reject automatically; AI match grades only sort | Workday, Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters, Oracle, Indeed, LinkedIn docs |

## Waiting

| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Time to first interview | About 5.6 days on average, for applications that get one | Huntr, 2025 |
| Interview to offer | About 12 days median | Huntr, 2025 |
| Ghosting after an interview | 61% of job seekers report it | Greenhouse survey, 2024 |
| Follow-ups | In recruiters' own outreach, replies rise from 8.3% after one email to 15.8% after two | Gem, 2022. Inference only: no study measures job seekers' follow-ups |

## Interviewing

| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Structured interviews | The most predictive hiring method measured (validity .42): answers scored against set questions | Sackett et al., meta-analysis, 2022 |
| Practice | Candidates with 5+ prior interviews passed phone screens more often (e.g. 71% vs 40% at one company) | interviewing.io, self-selected survey |
| Thank-you notes | 80% of HR managers call them helpful; no study measures their effect on outcomes | Robert Half survey, 2017 |

## Offers

| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Countering | People who countered gained about 12% in pay; nearly half didn't counter | Cullen et al., randomized study of 3,858 tech job seekers, 2025 |
| Pay ranges | 59% of US postings show pay | Indeed Hiring Lab, May 2025 |

## Folklore: never quote these as fact

- "75% of résumés are rejected by the ATS": a 2012 sales pitch from a résumé vendor, no data.
- "Recruiters spend 6 seconds": a vendor study with no published sample size.
- "250 résumés per job": a 2015 blog post with no data (it happens to match today's
  numbers, but say "about 240 per job on Greenhouse in 2025" instead).
