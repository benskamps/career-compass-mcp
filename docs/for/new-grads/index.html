Career Compass · for new grads

New grad? Make a short résumé count.

free · MIT · no account
a Claude plugin

A first résumé is short, and the pull is to make it sound longer. Career Compass works from what you did: an internship, a lab job, a capstone. It tells you which postings you're a real fit for, and when a draft would be stronger with a number you didn't give, it leaves a visible [confirm: …] for you to fill in instead of making one up.

Get it in the Claude directory

Two clicks, no setup

  1. In the Claude app or on claude.ai, open Customize → Plugins.
  2. Search for Career Compass and add it.
  3. Paste a job posting and your résumé, and ask Do I fit this?
claude.ai chat · web, desktop, mobileSkills only. Claude works from what you paste. Nothing is saved between chats.
